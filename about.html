About OceanGate

Good choices begin
with better information.

A considered approach to storage, from the first comparison to the help you need afterwards.

A company built around storage.

OceanGate Limited is the storage company in this educational project. The brief describes a business established three years earlier, with growing local and international demand driven by product quality, timely delivery and after-sales service.

Its focus spans floppy disks, optical media, compatible drives and archival tapes. As the range grows, customers need a clearer way to understand what each product does and where it fits.

Help before a decision.
Support after it.

A recommendation alone cannot tell you whether a disc format, drive interface or tape generation will work with your equipment. This catalogue brings those details together so you can inspect products, compare compatible options and download specifications for discussion.

Service-centre and distributor listings provide a direct route to guidance. Quality, delivery and support are commitments within the project scenario, rather than independently verified business claims.
