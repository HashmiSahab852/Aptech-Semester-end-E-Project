Before you choose

Compatibility, made clearer.

Find specification downloads ↗

Check the exact drive model, media format and supported generation. CD-R, CD-RW and DVD formats require matching drive support. A broad category alone does not establish compatibility.

Native capacity is the uncompressed amount. Compressed capacity assumes compressible data and a stated ratio; actual results vary. Do not rely on a compressed figure for already compressed files.

No. LightScribe requires compatible LightScribe media, a supporting writer and suitable software. Printable surfaces use a compatible disc printer; the two features are different.

Keep media in suitable cases away from excessive heat, moisture, dust and magnetic fields where relevant. Avoid touching disc surfaces. Follow the drive and media manufacturer care guidance.
